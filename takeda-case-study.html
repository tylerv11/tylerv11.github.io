Manufacturing Cycle Time Analytics

Ask four teams at a manufacturing site how long a single step takes, and you can get four different answers. This case study walks through how I tackled that problem: getting those teams to agree on one definition, turning raw machine signals into a trusted process-step record, and building a dashboard, a delay comment app, and an AI assistant pattern on top of it.

I chose this project because it's a good example of a messy problem that needed more than one tool. The site had trouble defining and agreeing on its measures, and the data lived in two different worlds: legacy on-premises systems and a modern Databricks lakehouse that couldn't connect to each other directly yet. Part of the work was bringing them together in one governed view that teams across the site could use. It also shows how I like to work: start with the people, agree on the words, then build.

About this example: This is an executive-level overview based on my manufacturing analytics work.
