My Life
outside of work.

Yangmingshan Park, Taiwan

Hi, I'm Tyler. I think best in systems: how data moves, where trust breaks down, and what has to be true before a team can make a better decision. I taught myself how to build this site the same way I approach analytics work: by taking the system apart, understanding the pieces, and rebuilding it into something clearer.

I grew up in Dallas in a single-parent household and moved to Los Angeles to study Industrial & Systems Engineering at the University of Southern California. USC gave me a technical foundation, but it also taught me how much good engineering depends on writing, listening, and translating complex ideas for people with different priorities.

Some of my favorite memories came from moments of connection: stargazing with an astronomy professor in the Anza-Borrego desert, grilling burgers with my Christian fraternity to serve people experiencing homelessness on Friday nights, and learning from people whose backgrounds and daily realities were very different from mine. That community helped me build purpose, belonging, and resilience as both an individual and a leader.

Outside work, I like fishing, video editing, off-roading, cooking, and surfing. Lately I've also gotten into 3D printing and graphic design, making pieces for friends and family as a creative outlet that blends the technical and artistic sides of how I think.
